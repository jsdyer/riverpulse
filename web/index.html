Sources & method

From Canadian gauges

Station metadata, recent unit observations and archived daily means come from ECCC's MSC GeoMet API. Every series includes its exact source query in the exported receipt. Data source: Environment and Climate Change Canada and the station contributors shown above. ECCC data licence. The scheduled collection starts with Langley, Hope and Bedford; the search covers the full catalogue.

Read the measure carefully

Discharge is volume per second (m³/s). Water level is metres relative to a station's datum; it is neither river depth nor a comparable flood-risk scale between gauges. Datum changes can also affect long-term comparisons. Recent values are provisional and can be revised. Gaps stay empty.

Historical curves align calendar days, retaining February 29. They compare daily means with daily means, separately from recent unit readings. Missing records and different sampling coverage can affect comparisons.

Your data & the source

Station searches use the downloaded catalogue. “Fetch this station” contacts ECCC directly. Imports stay in memory; nothing is uploaded or saved by this tool. Download a receipt to keep it. The site may count visits through CoolCode's shared metrics.
